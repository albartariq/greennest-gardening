Who We Are

GreenNest is a plant-loving community and store based in Karachi, dedicated to helping people create beautiful green spaces in their homes and gardens. Whether you're a first-time plant parent or a seasoned gardener, we're here to make your journey simple and joyful.

From indoor plants and outdoor greens to gardening tools and expert care tips, we provide everything you need to bring a little nature into your everyday life.
